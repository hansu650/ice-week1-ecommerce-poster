# Source record: China Digital Trade Development Report 2025

## Bibliographic record

- Chinese title: 《中国数字贸易发展报告2025》
- English working title: *China Digital Trade Development Report 2025*
- Publisher: Ministry of Commerce of the People's Republic of China (MOFCOM)
- Publication year: 2025
- Official landing page: https://fms.mofcom.gov.cn/xxfb/art/2025/art_3aeb2e47113845bdb16dcb053e5f6e9d.html
- Accessed: 2026-09-01
- Relevant evidence: Table 11, “2018-2024年中国跨境电子商务进出口总体情况表”, printed page 63 / PDF page 68
- Original data source named by the report: General Administration of Customs of China (GACC)

## Evidence chain

The included CSV is a manual transcription of the seven annual rows in Table 11. Headers were standardised to English snake case, and blank 2018 year-on-year values remain blank. The plot script checks that each annual total equals exports plus imports before generating the committed PNG and SVG.

The editable XLSX is derived from the CSV. It adds formula-based export and import shares and an editable chart; it is not a separate source.

## Interpretation limits

- The values are customs-recorded cross-border e-commerce imports and exports in current RMB 100 million.
- They are not total domestic e-commerce sales, platform gross merchandise value, or global retail sales.
- The chart describes growth and composition; it does not establish that cross-border e-commerce caused changes in foreign retailers.
- Comparisons with UNCTAD or other international datasets require reconciliation of definitions, coverage, taxes, currency, and reporting year.
