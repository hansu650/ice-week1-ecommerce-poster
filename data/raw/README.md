# Raw-data provenance

- `china_cbec_trade_2018_2024_verified.csv` is a transcription of Table 11 in the Ministry of Commerce's *China Digital Trade Development Report 2025*. The table attributes the data to the General Administration of Customs of China.
- `ipc_purchase_source_china_2016_2023.csv` preserves all eight published annual observations from HKTDC slide 8, with IPC survey-scope notes. No values are interpolated.
- `rcep_trade_potential_by_country_year.csv` preserves the paper's Table 19 country-by-year model ratios used by the poster.
- `unctad_business_ecommerce_figure2_vector_points_2016_2022.csv` records the seven y-coordinates of the blue central-estimate vector path in UNCTAD Figure 2 plus its 0 and 5 trillion axis calibration coordinates. Box 1 states that Figure 2's central estimate matches the overall total in Figure 1.

Official URLs, page references, access dates, licences, and limitations are recorded in [`../../DATA_SOURCES.md`](../../DATA_SOURCES.md).
